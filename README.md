# Sales-Performance-Analysis



Introduction
This project focuses on analyzing a sales dataset containing 370 sales records with information about customers, orders, products, salespersons, locations, regions, shipping details, payment methods, and product categories.

The analysis was conducted to understand overall sales performance, identify high-performing regions and salespersons, examine product revenue, and observe revenue trends over time.

The results were presented through an interactive Sales Performance Analysis dashboard, which provides a visual summary of key sales performance indicators.





Project Objective
The main objective of this project is to analyze sales data and generate useful business insights that can support sales and operational decision-making.

Specific objectives include:
Determine the total revenue generated.
Analyze the quantity of products purchased.
Identify the highest-performing sales region.
Identify salespersons generating the highest revenue.
Determine products contributing significantly to revenue.
Examine monthly revenue trends.
Compare regional sales performance.
Identify patterns that can support future sales planning and resource allocation.

Project Structure
The project followed the following analytical structure:

1. Data Collection
The sales dataset containing 370 rows and 22 columns was used as the primary data source.

2. Data Preparation
The dataset was reviewed to understand its structure, including customer, order, product, salesperson, regional, payment, and shipping information.

3. Exploratory Analysis
The data was examined to identify sales patterns, revenue distribution, product performance, salesperson performance, and regional differences.

4. Data Visualization
Key findings were transformed into charts and KPI cards using the sales performance dashboard.

5. Dashboard Analysis
The dashboard was used to interpret overall performance and compare different business dimensions.

6. Insight Generation
The findings were converted into business insights and observations that can support sales planning and performance monitoring.

Post-Analysis Observations and Recommendations


Observations
Strong overall sales performance: The dataset generated approximately 435.04K in total revenue from 20,592 units purchased.


North leads regional revenue: The North region generated approximately 141.66K, making it the largest contributor among the four regions shown.


Differences in salesperson performance: Nancy Freehafer recorded the highest revenue among the salespersons highlighted, with approximately 104.25K.


Coffee is a major revenue contributor: Coffee generated approximately 75.5K, making it one of the strongest-performing products displayed.


Monthly revenue fluctuates: Revenue varies across the months, with December recording the highest displayed monthly revenue at approximately 67K.


Regional performance is relatively distributed: Although North leads, the South, East, and West regions also make substantial contributions to overall revenue.




Recommendations


Strengthen high-performing regions: Analyze the strategies and customer patterns in the North region and identify practices that could potentially be applied to other regions.


Support salespersons with lower performance: Review differences in salesperson performance and provide targeted training, coaching, or sales support where necessary.


Maintain availability of high-performing products: Ensure that products generating substantial revenue, particularly Coffee, are adequately stocked to reduce the possibility of missed sales.


Investigate monthly sales patterns: Examine the reasons for high and low monthly revenue and use the findings to improve sales planning and inventory management.


Develop regional sales strategies: Create strategies based on the specific customer and product characteristics of each region rather than applying the same approach across all regions.


Conduct deeper product analysis: Further analyze product categories, quantity sold, pricing, and revenue to identify products with strong sales volume as well as products with strong revenue contribution.


Monitor performance regularly: Use the dashboard as a recurring performance-monitoring tool so that changes in revenue, products, regions, and salesperson performance can be identified early.







Conclusion

The Sales Performance Analysis provides an overview of revenue, product demand, salesperson contribution, regional performance, and monthly sales patterns.


The analysis shows approximately 435.04K in total revenue and 20,592 units purchased. The North region contributes the largest regional share, while Nancy Freehafer is the highest-performing salesperson highlighted in the dashboard. Coffee is also identified as a major revenue-generating product.


The monthly trend demonstrates that sales performance changes throughout the year, with December recording the highest displayed monthly revenue of approximately 67K.


Overall, the dashboard provides a useful framework for monitoring sales performance and identifying areas that require further investigation. Future analysis could examine customer behavior, product categories, pricing, discounts, and regional factors to understand the drivers behind the observed revenue patterns.


The dashboard labels some KPIs as “Q1”, while the monthly trend displays January through December. For a final portfolio submission, it would be useful to make the period labels consistent — for example, clearly stating whether the 435.04K KPI represents Q1 or the full analyzed period.
